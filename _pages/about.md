# 王浩轩 (Haoxuan Wang) 

I am a prospective PhD student in the [**HDVIS Lab**](https://www.hdvis.net/) at Hangzhou Dianzi University (HDU), under the guidance of [**Prof. Zhiguang Zhou**](https://www.hdvis.net/). My current work explores how to integrate large models with visual and multimodal reasoning, aiming to improve the interpretability and efficiency of intelligent systems in real-world applications.
